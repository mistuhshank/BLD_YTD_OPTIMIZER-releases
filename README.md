# BLD_YTD_OPTIMIZER - downloads

GTA V / FiveM YTD catalog, linker and optimizer: search every texture in the game's YTDs, link your cars to the YTDs that already hold their textures, and remove the duplicates from your packs.

**Download:** open [Releases](../../releases/latest) and get the newest `BLD_YTD_OPTIMIZER-<version>-x64.msi`.

**Access:** after installing, click **Log in with Discord**. The tool opens for accounts with the **ytd-tool-customer** role (after purchase) or the **ytd-tool-tester** role in the BLD Discord server. Get access at the [BLD store](https://black-line-development.tebex.io/category/bld-dev-utilities).

**Your repos:** the YTD repo and vanilla metas are not included. The tool creates `Documents\BLD_YTD_OPTIMIZER` with `YTD_REPO`, `VANILLA_METAS` and `CUSTOM_TEXTURES` folders; follow the BLD guide to fill them.

Windows 10 or 11 (64-bit). Everything else, including .NET, is in the installer.

`latest.json` is read by the app to tell you when a newer version is available.

*BLD_YTD_OPTIMIZER is an independent tool and is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive.*
